What AI Overviews Are

In Plain English

Google now often shows an AI-written summary at the very top of search results, above the normal list of websites. To write it, Google pulls from pages it trusts on the topic and links them as sources. Being cited there puts a brand in front of searchers before any regular result — it's the newest form of search visibility.

How I Measured It

1

Used Search Console's AI performance report to see how often Elite IIT pages appeared in AI Overviews: 10,986 impressions in August, 14.7% of the month's search impressions.

2

Identified the pages AI Overviews cited most often, to see which content Google's AI draws on — in-depth, well-structured pages with clear answers.

3

Compared overall Search Console performance for April and August to see how visibility moved across the whole site.

Verified Live, Not Just in a Dashboard

Monthly averages can hide what's really happening, so I also checked the branded searches directly.

✓

On September 7, I searched every branded term — "Elite IIT", "Elite IIT Malleshwaram" and the other branches — in an incognito window, so personal history couldn't affect results. Every one ranked #1.

A Related Finding — What Drives Local Rankings

  • Review velocity matters more than star rating. A competitive check of top-ranking coaching institutes showed Malleshwaram outranks even higher-rated competitors by consistently generating new reviews — about 2 a day.
  • Complete Business Profiles rank better. Branches with nearly all service listings filled in (83 of 85) improved their Google Maps rankings.

Why This Matters Going Forward

Search is shifting toward AI-generated answers — sometimes called AEO (Answer Engine Optimization). Ranking in the regular results is no longer the only way to be seen; being the source Google's AI quotes is becoming just as important. Deep, well-structured course pages with clear answers are the kind of content AI answers draw on.

The bottom line: 10,986 AI Overview impressions in a single month, 37.9% more search impressions, 274 queries improved, and every branded search confirmed at #1.
