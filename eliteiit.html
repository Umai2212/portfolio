Full-Stack Digital Marketing for Elite IIT Coaching

Running digital marketing for a 4-branch coaching institute in Bengaluru since May 2026 — restructured paid campaigns, rebuilt organic and local search, and set up lead tracking across Meta Ads, Google Ads, SEO and Google Business Profile.

ClientElite IIT Coaching, Bengaluru
RoleDigital Marketing Executive — SEO & Paid Ads
PeriodMay 2026 – Present
ChannelsMeta Ads · Google Ads · SEO · GBP · WordPress

The Situation

Elite IIT runs 4 branches across Bengaluru — Yelahanka, Jayanagar, Koramangala and Malleshwaram — offering JEE, NEET and Class 3–12 tuition. They had been using freelancers and hired a full-time digital marketer for the first time.

Starting Point

  • One generic Meta campaign with no product segmentation or lead qualification
  • Facebook page restricted — 97% of ad delivery on Instagram only
  • NEET page at rank ~24 with 2 organic visits a month
  • Google Business Profiles missing Class 3–12 tuition across all 4 branches
  • No lead tracking or reporting in place
  • Website loading in ~7 seconds

What Was Expected

  • More qualified leads reaching the counselling team
  • Local search visibility for all 4 branches
  • Proper tracking and reporting with real numbers
  • A digital marketing function that runs independently

Meta Ads

The previous setup had a single NEET campaign with no segmentation and no qualification layer. I restructured the account into separate campaigns per product, geo-targeted per branch, with a WhatsApp qualification filter screening leads before they reached the counselling team.

What I Did

  • Restructured from 1 generic campaign into segmented campaigns per product (NEET, Class 6–12, Navodaya, Dropper)
  • Set up geo-targeting per branch — Yelahanka, Koramangala, Jayanagar, Malleshwaram
  • Built a WhatsApp qualification filter — screening questions before a lead reaches a counsellor
  • Changed age targeting from 28–45 to 18–45 to reach students directly alongside parents
  • Built audience segments: website visitors, interest-based (NEET, JEE, coaching), lookalikes
  • Launched the NEET Crash Course campaign the same day the re-exam was announced — in-house copy, 4 creatives, landing page live within the week, 104 leads
  • Set up a new Meta dataset (Pixel + Conversions API), replacing a setup that had undercounted 100+ leads
  • Launched landing-page lead-form campaigns alongside WhatsApp campaigns

SEO — From Invisible to Compounding Growth

The NEET page was getting 2 organic visits a month at rank ~24 in May. Rebuilding it was the start of a broader SEO effort across 35+ course pages.

What Was Built

  • 35+ course pages created and optimised — Navodaya, Sainik, RMS, IBPS, CET, NEET and more, with multiple lead forms per page
  • NEET page: grew from 2,200 to 4,800 words, hub-and-spoke structure, FAQ schema, internal linking
  • Site speed: traced high impressions with almost no clicks to a ~7-second load time
  • Schema markup: Course schema across course pages
  • Technical audits: Screaming Frog crawls; corrected meta tags on 42 of 43 flagged pages
  • Authority: audited the existing citation profile and started education-niche guest-post outreach

Infrastructure Built

None of the following were requested. Built because they didn't exist.

  • Lead tracking sheet — full pipeline visibility for the team; it caught pop-up form leads landing in a spam folder, which were then recovered
  • 18-month historical campaign analysis — a clear baseline for future comparisons
  • WhatsApp qualification filter — screens leads before the counselling team
  • Monthly execution calendar — shared with management for visibility
  • Geo-targeting circles — Yelahanka and Koramangala built in Google Maps
  • EOD reporting system — daily report sent every evening

Key Learnings

  • Audit before you optimise — the right comparison changes the whole picture
  • Qualification matters more than volume — screening leads is worth more than adding more
  • Tracking is the foundation — a new Meta dataset recovered 100+ leads the old setup wasn't counting
  • Local SEO compounds — consistent Business Profile work put 77 keywords in the Maps top 10
  • CPL means nothing without outcomes — track leads through to visits and admissions
  • Review velocity beats star rating — a finding from direct competitive GBP analysis
  • Say what you can't verify — where earlier data mixed metric types, I don't show a before/after
