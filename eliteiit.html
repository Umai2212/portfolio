Full-Stack Digital Marketing for Elite IIT Coaching

Joined as sole digital marketing manager for a 4-branch coaching institute in Bangalore — audited a broken setup, rebuilt paid, organic, and local search from the ground up, and ran it end-to-end for 4+ months across Meta Ads, Google Ads, SEO, and Google Business Profile.

ClientElite IIT Coaching, Bangalore
RoleDigital Marketing Manager
PeriodMay 2026 – Present
ChannelsMeta Ads · Google Ads · SEO · GBP · WordPress

The Situation

Elite IIT runs 4 branches across Bangalore — Yelahanka, Jayanagar, Koramangala, and Malleshwaram — offering JEE, NEET, and Class 3–12 tuition. They had been using freelancers and hired a full-time manager for the first time.

When I joined: Meta Ads running with no qualification filters and an inflated lead count, Google Ads conversion tracking broken since launch, GBP profiles at position 16+ city-wide, the highest-selling product (Class 3–12 tuition) with zero digital presence, and an internal narrative that performance had dropped — based on numbers that turned out to be misleading.

What I Walked Into

  • 18-month Meta Ads average CPL of ₹65.91 — inflated by unqualified leads
  • Facebook page restricted — 97% delivery on Instagram only
  • Google Ads showing 14 form submissions with zero emails received — tracking broken since launch
  • NEET page at rank ~24 with 2,200 words and 2 organic visits/month
  • GBP profiles unoptimised — Class 3–12 tuition missing across all 4 branches
  • No leads tracking, no reporting infrastructure
  • Website loading in 7 seconds — 1,160 impressions/day but only 14 clicks

What Was Expected

  • Lower CPL than the previous freelancer's ₹53.37
  • More qualified leads reaching the counseling team
  • Local SEO visibility across all 4 branches
  • Proper tracking and reporting with real numbers
  • A digital marketing function that runs independently

Meta Ads

The previous setup had a single NEET campaign with no segmentation and no qualification layer. Internal comparisons had also been mixing Navodaya, School Tuition, and job-applicant leads into one "NEET leads" bucket — the comparison being used internally was inaccurate.

I restructured the account into separate campaigns per product, geo-targeted per branch, with a WhatsApp qualification filter screening leads before they reached the counseling team — then ran that approach at increasing scale over the following months.

On measurement honesty: before I joined, Meta results were tracked inconsistently — phone calls, messaging conversations, and leads were treated interchangeably depending on the campaign, so a clean admissions-based before/after isn't something I can stand behind. Counsellor-reported lead-to-admission conversion before my qualification filter was in the rough 15–25% range, unverified. Cost-per-lead is the one metric that was tracked consistently throughout, which is why that's the number I lead with.

What I Did

  • Restructured from 1 generic campaign into segmented campaigns per product (NEET, Class 6–12, Navodaya, Dropper)
  • Set up geo-targeting per branch — Yelahanka, Koramangala, Jayanagar, Malleshwaram
  • Built a WhatsApp qualification filter — screening questions before a lead reaches a counselor
  • Changed age targeting from 28–45 to 18–45 to capture students directly alongside parents
  • Built audience segments: website visitors, interest-based (NEET, JEE, coaching), lookalikes
  • Launched the NEET Crash Course campaign the same day the re-exam was announced — in-house copy, 4 creatives approved, landing page live within the week, 104 leads generated
  • Sustained and scaled the qualification-first approach over 4 months, not just the launch month

Google Ads

Tracking Fix

Rakesh flagged at 8:55 PM on June 10 that Google Ads was showing 14 form submissions but no emails had been received. I responded at 5:01 AM June 11 and had the root cause diagnosed and fixed by 12:04 PM — 15 hours total.

Root Cause: The lead form asset was collecting submissions but no conversion event was firing and email notifications had never been configured. This had been broken since the account launched — nobody had caught it until the flag came in.

  • Implemented Thank You page redirect as the conversion signal
  • Configured Google Tag Manager to fire the conversion event on thank you page load
  • Set test campaign at ₹200/day to verify end-to-end before scaling

SEO — From Invisible to Compounding Growth

The NEET page was getting 2 organic visits a month at rank ~24 in May. Rebuilding it was the start of a broader SEO effort that ran continuously through September.

What Was Rebuilt

  • NEET page: grew from 2,200 to 4,800 words, hub-and-spoke structure, FAQ schema, internal linking
  • Malleshwaram: built a dedicated location page, optimised GBP — moved to #2 Search / #4 Maps within a month, from no ranking at all
  • Site speed: identified a 7-second page load time as the cause of high impressions (1,160/day) with almost no clicks (14)
  • Schema markup: implemented Course schema across course pages
  • Technical audits: ran site crawls via Screaming Frog; corrected meta tags on 42 of 43 flagged pages

The Structural Fix — Site Architecture

The site had generic location pages ("Coaching in Yelahanka") and generic course pages ("NEET Coaching in Bangalore") — but was missing the intersection of the two. That single gap explained most of the "not ranking" results across School Tuition, Navodaya/Sainik/RMS, and CLAT programs.

Competitive check (Sept 2026): A follow-up 9-institute competitor audit confirmed the diagnosis independently — Elite IIT's Domain Rating (7) is competitive with 4 of 7 real competitors reviewed. The gap isn't authority, it's page structure and trust signals. This audit is recommended and awaiting internal sign-off, not yet executed.

What Drives GBP Rank Here

Review velocity — how frequently new reviews come in — turned out to be a stronger ranking driver than star rating alone. Malleshwaram outranks higher-rated competitors by generating roughly 2 reviews a day, consistently.

Leads to Revenue — Why Cost-Per-Lead Isn't the Real Metric

Cost per lead is a useful operational number, but it doesn't tell you whether the business made money. August's data made that distinction concrete. Elite IIT needs 25–40 admissions a month to hit its ₹7–10L revenue target depending on season — 43 admissions closed in August, against that bar.

Catching a Blind Spot in the Tracking System

The August tracking data surfaced a bigger issue than lead quality: of 125 leads that month, only 25 (20.0%) were genuinely worked and lost. The remaining 56 (44.8%) never reached a real outcome at all — never forwarded, never claimed by a counsellor, or never updated after entry. The sales-process gap was larger than the conversion problem itself.

Infrastructure Built

None of the following were requested. Built proactively because they didn't exist.

  • Leads tracking sheet — full pipeline visibility shared with the whole team
  • 18-month historical campaign analysis — corrected the internal narrative that performance had dropped
  • WhatsApp qualification filter — screens leads before the counseling team
  • Monthly execution calendar — shared with Rakesh for visibility
  • Geo-targeting circles — Yelahanka and Koramangala built in Google Maps
  • EOD reporting system — daily report sent every evening

Key Learnings

  • Audit before you optimise — the internal numbers looked bad because the comparison was wrong
  • Qualification matters more than volume — removing unqualified leads is worth more than adding more
  • Tracking is the foundation — the Google Ads account had been spending with zero conversion signal for weeks
  • Local SEO compounds fast — one month of focused GBP + location page work moved Malleshwaram from invisible to #2
  • CPL means nothing without revenue context — same lead volume and CPL can mean very different business outcomes
  • Review velocity beats star rating — a finding from direct competitive GBP analysis, not assumption
  • Structural content gaps hide as ranking problems — a missing page type can look like an SEO failure until you check site architecture
  • Say what you can't verify — Meta's pre-hire tracking mixed metric types, so I report a clean cost-per-lead comparison instead of a fabricated admissions figure
